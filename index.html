Work, 2017–now

Júlia Nueno Guitart: investigations, tools, exhibitions and writing

I am a researcher and engineer. My work explores the digital systems that organise life and the spaces we inhabit, with the goal of developing new ways of understanding and acting with and through them. Currently, I am fellow researcher at Forensic Architecture, and I am pursuing a PhD at the Centre for Research Architecture, Goldsmiths, University of London.
